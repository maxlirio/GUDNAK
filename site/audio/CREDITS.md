# Soundtrack

Every track here is **CC0 / public domain** — free for any use, commercial or
not, with no attribution required. They are credited anyway, because the people
who made them are owed it whatever the licence says.

| In game | Track | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

*Breves Dies Hominis* is a 12th–13th century composition with Latin lyrics,
performed as a vocal solo.

Battle plays the tomb, then a cue, then the tomb, then the other cue, and round
again — about nine and a half minutes for a full turn. The ambience is the
ground the game sits on, but it never runs for more than three and a half
minutes at a time.

The files here are not the originals: each has been transcoded to 96 kbps MP3
and loudness-matched to -18 LUFS so the crossfades between them do not jump.
The originals (mixed MP3, Ogg and WAV, at mixed loudness) are at the links
above.

## One that was rejected

*Ancient Power of Serpents* was uploaded to OpenGameArt tagged CC0, and is a
good fit. It is by **Kevin MacLeod**, whose work is licensed **CC BY**, not
CC0 — the uploader was not its author and had no standing to relicense it. A
CC0 tag on a re-upload is a claim, not a licence. It was dropped.

CC0 does not require this file to exist. It exists because a licence saying you
need not credit somebody is not the same as a reason not to.
