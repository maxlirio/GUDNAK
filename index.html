The board

Nine squares. Deploy into your back row, push forward, and park a fighter on your opponent's Gates — every turn they start under siege they burn a card off their Stronghold. When it's empty, they lose.

  • 2 actions a turn — 1 on the very first turn — and you must spend them if you can.
  • Combat is power only. Higher power wins, a tie kills both, the winner slides in.
  • Stacks form by deploying onto a friendly fighter that shares a trait. Only the top one is in play, and power never adds up.
  • Defend costs an action and discards cards equal to the sieging fighter's power — then destroys it.

Progress

  • Rules engine — headless and deterministic. 400 games and 33,581 decisions of random-legal play with invariants asserted after every decision, zero violations.
  • Rules of record — the full ruleset, pulled out of the official rules app's JS bundle rather than a PDF, with every ambiguity marked as an explicit ruling.
  • Card pipeline — CARD_FORGE exports JSON, the ingest tool decodes it, the faces get read, the catalogue rebuilds itself.
  • 3D table — three.js, cards as objects on a lit board. Next up.
  • Online play — PeerJS room codes, no server.
  • Deck builder — waiting on the remaining faction card databases.
